Our Heritage

From Cloud-Forest Farms to Your Morning Ritual

Founded in 2018 by two lifelong friends obsessed with origin transparency, Aroma & Ember started with a simple conviction: great coffee shouldn't feel pretentious, and ethical sourcing shouldn't be optional.

We partner directly with family-run estates in Ethiopia's Yirgacheffe region, Colombia's Huila valley, and India's Chikmagalur hills—paying 45% above Fair Trade minimums to support regenerative shade-grown agriculture.
